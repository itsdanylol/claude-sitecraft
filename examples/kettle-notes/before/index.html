From cup to shelf in three steps

Write as you taste. Every note keeps its temperature, time and leaf weight, so next season you compare like with like.

  1. Log the steep

    Each infusion gets its own line, with the numbers chosen, not typed, and a few words of what you taste.

  2. Compare harvests

    Open two sessions of the same tea side by side and read them steep by steep.

  3. Print the card

    Keep the words worth keeping and print an A6 shelf card that fits beside the tin.

Shelf cards your customers will read

The card is written from your own tasting notes: what it tastes like, how to brew it, what it costs. Printed at A6, it stands beside the tin.
