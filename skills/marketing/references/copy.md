# Copy

## Lengths that hold

| Element | Characters | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `h1` | 28 to 64 | Two to nine words. One clause, or two at most. |
| Subhead | 80 to 160 | Adds the mechanism or the audience. Never repeats the `h1`. |
| Section heading | 20 to 48 | Names what the section proves, not what it says. |
| Section body | 120 to 420 | Two to five sentences. Past five, it is a page, not a section. |
| Button | 2 to 5 words | The outcome. "Buy license — $1,000" not "Click here to purchase". |
| Caption or label | 1 to 4 words | Sentence case. No trailing period. |
| Meta title | 50 to 60 | Product name at the end, separated by a middot or a dash. |
| Meta description | 140 to 160 | A sentence a person would read twice. Not a keyword list. |
| Alt text | under 125 | What the image shows and why it is here. |
| 404 | under 120 | What happened, where to go, one link. |

## Voice

Pick two or three words from the positioning block and stay inside them. If the character is "exact, unimpressed, a little dry", every sentence can be checked against that. A sentence that needs a different character to work is a different product's sentence.

Write to one person, second person, present tense. Active voice unless the actor is genuinely unknown. Contractions are fine and usually better. A fragment is fine for rhythm, once.

Read the paragraph aloud. If a word would never leave your mouth in a conversation with a colleague, it is a word from a template.

## What the first screen does

1. Says what the thing is, in words the reader already uses.
2. Says why it is different, in the mechanism.
3. Shows the product, not a metaphor for the product.
4. Offers one action.

If the first screen does any of these four things twice, cut the weaker one.

## Controls and microcopy

- **Buttons** name the result: "Start the run", "Book a call", "Read the terms". Not "Submit", "Continue", "Learn more".
- **Field labels** sit above the field, sentence case, no placeholder substituting for a label.
- **Errors** say what went wrong, which field, and what to do. "That email needs an @ sign" beats "Invalid input".
- **Empty states** say what will appear here and the one action that fills it.
- **Loading** says what is loading and roughly how long, if it is over a second.
- **Confirmations** are quiet and specific. "Saved. Next run picks this up." Not "Success! 🎉".
- **Destructive actions** name the object: "Delete contract ctr_3219", and say what cannot be undone.

## Pricing copy

Per plan: what it is, what it includes, what one click does, and the thing that surprises people. Ranked facts, not adjectives. If there is a limit, put the number in the same sentence as the price. If a run that finds nothing still costs money, say that before the button, not after it.

## Objections

Write the objection in the buyer's words, then one honest paragraph. Three is enough, and they belong near the price, not in a FAQ nobody opens. If the honest answer is "no", write no and say who it is for instead.

## Claim shapes

- **Measured.** "113 of 113 tools passing, checked before every release." Only with a date and a source.
- **Structural.** "Nothing runs until four gates pass." True by construction, and checkable by reading the code.
- **Comparative.** "Half the setup of a self-hosted scanner." Only against a named alternative you actually measured.
- **Testimonial.** One person, one result, one name, with permission. No initials-only quotes on a site that has no customers.

Anything not in one of these four shapes is decoration.

## Bans

Exclamation marks. "!", "!!". Hype words: supercharge, seamless, unlock, magic, revolutionize, game-changing, effortless, powerful, cutting-edge, next-generation, AI-powered, blazing fast, enterprise-grade, world-class. Greeting headlines. Uppercase tracked labels. "We're excited to announce". "Trusted by" with no source. "Coming soon" as a substitute for a date. Numbers without units. Percentages without a comparison. Trailing periods on anything under a sentence.

## Rewrites that show the rule

| Before | After | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Supercharge your workflow" | "Draft, edit and ship from one file" | Names the mechanism and the objects. |
| "Trusted by 10,000+ teams" | "Used by 41 teams, listed in the register" | A number someone can check. |
| "Seamless integration" | "One URL in your client, nothing installed" | Describes the actual install. |
| "Powerful analytics" | "One chart per question, no dashboard to configure" | Says what happens instead. |
| "Contact us for pricing" | "$1,000 per key, one full run, 25 assets" | Removes the click that wasted a day. |
| "Enter your email to get started!" | "Email" placeholder-free label, button "Get the sample" | Label stays a label, button names the outcome. |
